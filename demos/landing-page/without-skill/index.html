How it works

You set it up once. After that, nobody on the call has to take notes or write the follow-up.

  1. Connect your calendar

    Murmur looks for upcoming meetings that have a Zoom or Google Meet link. Those are the calls it will join.

  2. Murmur joins the call

    It shows up in the participant list as “Murmur”, so everyone can see the call is being recorded. Then it stays out of the way.

  3. The summary goes out

    When the call ends, everyone on the invite gets an email with the decisions, the to-dos, and a link to the recording.

An email you can read in thirty seconds

Most people don't want a transcript of a meeting they were just in. They want to know what changed and what they owe. So that's what the summary is.

Decisions
Written as plain sentences, so someone who wasn't there can tell what was agreed without asking around.
To-dos
Each one has a name next to it, and a date when one was mentioned. Nobody has to guess whose job it was.
Recording
Linked at the bottom for the times you need to hear exactly how something was said.
Who gets it
Everyone on the calendar invite, including the people who couldn't make it.

Questions

Do the people on my calls need a Murmur account?

No. Anyone on the calendar invite gets the summary email, whether or not they use Murmur. You only pay for the people who have Murmur join their calls.

What happens after my 10th call on the free plan?

Murmur stops joining calls for the rest of the month. Your count resets at the start of the next month, or you can upgrade to Team for unlimited calls.

Will people know the call is being recorded?

Yes. Murmur joins as a visible participant named “Murmur”, so it's in the participant list for the whole call. Some places require everyone's consent before a call is recorded, so check what applies to you and your attendees.

Which meeting apps does it work with?

Zoom and Google Meet.

When does the summary arrive?

After the call ends. Murmur waits until the meeting is over, then emails the summary to everyone on the invite.
