What happens on a call

  1. Before the call

    Paste the meeting link into Murmur, or connect your calendar and pick the calls you want it on.

  2. During the call

    Murmur joins like any other participant, under its own name, and records. It doesn’t talk or share its screen.

  3. When the call ends

    Everyone who was on the call gets the same email: a few lines on what was discussed, the decisions, the to-dos with the person who took each one, and a link to the recording.

Questions people ask first

Which meeting apps does it work with?
Zoom and Google Meet.
Will people on the call know it’s recording?
Yes. Murmur is in the participant list under its own name, the same as everyone else. The host can remove it like any other participant, and it stops recording when it leaves.
Who gets the email?
Everyone who was on the call. They don’t need a Murmur account to read it.
